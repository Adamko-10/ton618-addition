Educational labels

    For the game

    • Two presses (Adam, 15 November): the first at a star locks it, the second at the ground marks where the jet lands. The film starts on the second press.
    • The caster and players within about 128 blocks get the film. Everyone else sees only the world: the night turning to day and the jet far off.
    • The thin jet only lifts and carries. The radio lobe shoves and hurts what is inside it, more for bigger stars.
    • From tick 80 to 680 the film is our own full-screen space scene, so nothing in the world has to be drawn or synced there.
    • The second sun, the jet and the lobe are real world effects, timed from the cast: DAY 680, SWEEP 745, LOBE 830, POP 890, NIGHT 905.
    • Only shown when SS-21 Quasar is installed. Different from its strike: ours comes in low from the horizon, carries things along, and ends in a dome and crystals.
