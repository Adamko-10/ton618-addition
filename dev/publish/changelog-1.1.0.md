**For Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge.** Install on the server and every client. Needs **TON 618 1.1.0** and **RimuLib 1.2.3** or newer. TA-02 also needs **SS-21 Quasar**. The other loaders get 1.1.0 in a later update.

## 1.1.0: TA-02 · Quasar Jets

*   **TA-02 · Quasar Jets** (only with **SS-21 Quasar** installed): at night, hold TON 618's item (or the Stellar Remote) with TA-02 picked and eight named stars appear in the sky: Barnard's Star, Wolf 359, Alpha Centauri A, Tau Ceti, Rigel, Spica, Betelgeuse and Antares. Cast at one to lock it, then aim at the ground and cast again.
*   **What happens**: the remote fast-forwards the star's own time while a star engine drives it into TON618. TON618 tears it apart, lights up as a quasar, and fires a jet at the Earth. A **second sun** turns the night to day, the jet sweeps down carrying mobs and items, and it **lands in a huge blast**: a crater up to 60 blocks wide (by the star's type, from red dwarf to red supergiant), debris flung out, trees flattened and fires round the rim. A **radio lobe** swells over the crater and pops, and **Frozen Light** crystals in the star's colour are left in it (minable). The star is gone from the sky for a few nights.
*   **A landing marker** everyone near can see: a target as wide as the crater on the ground, a pillar of light standing on it, and a ring that pulses faster and faster until the jet lands. Before the second cast, the target shows where it would land and how big the crater will be (red when you're too close).
*   **Its own film**, and **37 new sounds**.
*   **Shader packs** (Iris): the stars, the second sun, the jet, the blast, the lobe and the marker are drawn the way the shader pack draws its own glowing things, so they sit in the world (hidden behind hills and trees, with the pack's glow) instead of looking pasted on. Tested with Complementary Reimagined.
*   **Educational mode** now also gives you **more time to read**: in a strike cast with it on, the parts with text slow down for everyone watching (TA-02 and Last Light).
*   **Server settings** for TA-02: turn it off, operators only, terrain changes, the second sun, carrying players, the most things carried, and how many nights a star stays missing.
