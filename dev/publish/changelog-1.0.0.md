## 1.0.0: the first release

*   **TA-01 · Last Light**: after a no-limit TON 618 has devoured the world, cast it in the dark to fast-forward time until TON618 evaporates by Hawking radiation. Its last light brings the world back for everyone in the dark, on a new morning.
*   **Its own film**: the universe cooling, the last stars going out, Hawking pairs at the horizon, the final burst and the world's return, with a year counter racing to 10¹⁰⁰. Everyone in the dark sees it.
*   **Educational mode** (off by default): small labels in the film pointing at the real physics. Switch it in the H menu's **TON 618 Addition** panel (key **\\**).
*   **How to unlock it** is shown in the H menu until it's ready.
*   On **TON 618's own item** and, with The Shooting Star installed, on the **Stellar Remote** too.
*   **Server settings**: turn Last Light off, or let only operators cast it.
