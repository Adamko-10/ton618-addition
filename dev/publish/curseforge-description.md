# TON 618 Addition

An **unofficial**, fan-made addon for **[TON 618](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ton618)** by rimuru\_dev. It adds **TA-01 · Last Light**: after TON 618 has devoured the world, fast-forward time by a googol of years until TON618 evaporates by **Hawking radiation**, and its last light brings the world back.

## TA-01 · Last Light

**How to unlock it** (the H menu shows these steps too):

1.  Set TON618's **Scale** to **∞** (no limit) in the H menu.
2.  **Use TON618**: it devours the world, and everyone ends up in the dark beside it.
3.  In the dark, pick **Last Light** in the H menu and **right-click** with TON 618 (or the Stellar Remote).

Then time lets go. A wave of light rolls out from you, and a film carries you through the future of the universe, with a year counter racing towards 10¹⁰⁰:

*   **The universe cools** below TON618's own temperature, and from then on the black hole starts to lose mass.
*   **The last stars go out**, about 10¹⁴ years from now; then the long dark of the degenerate era.
*   **The black-hole era**: pairs of particles appear at the horizon; one falls in, the other escapes, and TON618 slowly shrinks. That is **Hawking radiation**.
*   **Smaller, hotter, faster**: the smaller it gets, the hotter it burns and the faster it goes, until after a googol of years it ends in a **burst of light**.
*   **The world returns**: a second wave clears the white, and you are home again, on a new morning.

Everyone in the dark sees the whole film and comes back with you. If your films are switched off (RimuLib's film key, **J**), a small card shows the countdown instead.

## Educational mode

Switch it on in the H menu (the **TON 618 Addition** panel, top right, key **\\**): small labels in the film point at the real physics behind each moment (Hawking radiation, the eras of the universe, why smaller means hotter, the information paradox). Off by default.

## Settings

*   `config/ton618_addition-common.toml` (the server's): Last Light on or off, **who can cast it** (everyone, or operators only: it brings the world back for everyone, which TON 618 leaves to operators with `/ton618 restore`), and whether the world comes back on a morning.
*   `config/ton618_addition-client.toml` (each player's own): educational mode. Also under Mods → TON 618 Addition → Config.

## Will it work with future versions of TON 618?

It joins TON 618 through RimuLib's official addon API and TON 618's own `/ton618` commands, so small updates should keep working. If TON 618 changes how its devoured world works, wait for an update of this addon.
