## 1.1.2: fixes, and TA-02 on every loader

*   **Messages in TON 618's dark**: in the devoured world TON 618 hides the whole HUD, the action bar too, so when a skill couldn't be cast there you never saw why (TA-02's "only in the overworld, at night", for example). Now the action bar shows in the dark; the hotbar and chat stay hidden, as TON 618 wants.
*   **The H menu in the dark** also shows when another mod changes how the game draws its HUD.
*   **Default keys**: TA-01 · Last Light's **;** (on TON 618's item) and **'** (on the Stellar Remote), and TA-02 · Quasar Jets' **,** and **.**, never existed. Now they do. **If they show as unbound** in Options > Controls (likely if you also play with The Shooting Star Addition: SA Series), press **Reset** next to each.
*   **TA-02 · Quasar Jets and all of 1.1.x are now on every loader**: Forge 1.21.1 and 1.20.1, Fabric 1.21.1, 1.20.1, 1.21.11 and 26.3.
