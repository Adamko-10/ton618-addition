**For Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge.** Install on the server and every client. Needs **TON 618 1.1.0** and **RimuLib 1.2.3** or newer. TA-02 also needs **SS-21 Quasar**. The other loaders get TA-02 in a later update.

## 1.1.1: TA-02 · Quasar Jets polish

*   **A new landing**: the quasar's plasma hitting the Earth instead of a plain fireball. A blue-white flash, the jet's plasma splashing out over the ground, a ball of plasma that burns white-hot, then electric violet with threads of light, **magnetic arcs** leaping out of the crater and snapping, and a ring of cloud rising over it all. Standing inside the blast, you're bathed in its glow.
*   **A new impact sound**: an electric crack and a deep boom with a rolling rumble. Further off you hear a deeper, muffled boom, and it **reaches you at the speed of sound**: the flash first, then the boom.
*   **Power and Damage sliders**, like The Shooting Star's own skills, under TA-02 in the H menu. **Power**: 0.1× to 3× the star's own blast (a warning above 2×); it sizes the crater, the debris, the reach, the landing marker and the explosion. **Damage**: the hit at the blast's middle; **∞**, the default, kills everything in its reach.
*   **A readout for watching without the film** (films off, or further off than the film reaches): the phase, the star's racing clock, the time to impact, the mark's distance and direction, the blast's size, and a countdown as the jet comes in; afterwards the crater, the lobe, the frozen light and when the star is back.
*   **Without SS-21 Quasar**, TA-02 now shows in the menus, **locked**, instead of being hidden.
*   **Smoother landing**: the debris is thrown over a few moments instead of all at once.
*   **Server settings**: `max_power` (the most Power players may pick) and `max_damage` (a cap on the hit, so ∞ doesn't kill outright), under `[quasar_jets]`.
*   The film's TON 618 card now says **about 40 billion Suns** (newer estimates).
